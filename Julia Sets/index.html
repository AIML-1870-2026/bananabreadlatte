What are Julia Sets?

Julia sets are fractals generated by the formula z = z² + c, where c is a complex number. For each point on the canvas, we repeatedly apply this formula and color it based on how quickly it "escapes" to infinity.

How to explore:

  • Drag on the canvas to pan around
  • Scroll to zoom in and out
  • Adjust the Real (a) and Imaginary (b) sliders to change the shape
  • Try different color palettes for new vibes
  • Click a preset to jump to a curated fractal
  • Hit Surprise Me for something random
  • Export your creation as a high-res PNG
